Plan Coordinator
Coordinator only. This page holds the family signing key. Use it on one trusted device. Family members use the main app.

0 · Region set-up (required)

Defines which state or territory the household is in. It sets the state emergency, fire, health, power, water and gas numbers, and is sealed into the signed plan so family phones can rely on it.

1 · Full plan (send by email or MMS attachment)

Edit the plan as JSON. Contacts, checklists and procedures are addressed by their id. Publishing signs a new version.

2 · Quick update (send by SMS)

Small signed changes that fit in one or a few SMS messages. They apply on top of the last published plan.

Signed output

Open SMS Open email

Send full plans as an attachment (the .fcr file). Send quick updates as SMS text. Family apps reject anything not signed by this key, so the channel itself does not need to be trusted.
